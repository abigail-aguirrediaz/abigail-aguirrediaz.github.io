Fresas Con Crema

Fresas con Crema, or Mexican strawberries and cream, is the easiest dessert ever! Made with fresh strawberries and a light sweetened condensed milk sauce for a tasty and sweet snack or dessert.

Ingredients & Materials

  • 1 pound fresh strawberries
  • 2 cups sour cream
  • 1 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • ½ cup evaporated milk
  • Spoons/ Whisk
  • Serving Glass
  • Bowls or Cups

Serving & Preperation

  1. Prep: 10 minutes
  2. Servings: 6 servings

Instructions

  1. Remove and discard the stems from the strawberries. Slice the fruit into thin slices and transfer to a medium bowl.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, add sour cream, sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk. Whisk together to combine.
  3. Scoop ½ cup sliced strawberries into 6 bowls or cups. Then top each cup with ½ cup sweet cream mixture.
  4. Serve immediately with a spoon or store in the fridge for up to 5 days.
