Step 4: Chill & Serve | Paso cuatro

The final step is chilling the horchata and preparing it to serve. This helps the flavors fully blend together and creates the refreshing taste horchata is known for.

Instructions:

  1. Place the horchata in the refrigerator and chill for at least 1–2 hours.
  2. Stir the horchata well before serving because ingredients may settle at the bottom.
  3. Pour the horchata over ice in a glass.
  4. Optionally, sprinkle ground cinnamon on top for extra flavor and decoration.

Serving Tips-

Horchata is best served cold on a warm day. It pairs well with many Mexican dishes and is commonly enjoyed during family meals, celebrations, and gatherings.

Final Result-

After chilling, your homemade horchata should have a smooth texture, creamy flavor, and refreshing cinnamon taste. Enjoy your finished drink — ¡buen provecho!
