Step 2: Blending & Straining

After the rice mixture has finished soaking, the next step is blending and straining the ingredients to create the smooth base of the horchata.

Instructions

  1. Pour the soaked rice, cinnamon sticks, and soaking water into a blender.
  2. Blend the mixture until the rice becomes finely broken down.
  3. Add additional water gradually while blending to help create a smoother consistency.
  4. Place a fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth over a large bowl or pitcher.
  5. Slowly pour the blended mixture through the strainer.

Why Straining Is Important

Straining removes large rice pieces and cinnamon particles from the drink. This helps create the smooth and creamy texture that authentic horchata is known for.

Repeat for a Smoother Texture

For the best results, strain the horchata mixture more than once. Repeating this step helps remove extra graininess and creates a cleaner, smoother drink.
