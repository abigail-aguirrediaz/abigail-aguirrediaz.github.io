Our Story | Nuestra Historia

"La Receta de Mi Mamá” is more than just a recipe website — it is a way for me to preserve a piece of my culture, my family, and the love my mom has always shown through cooking. One recipe that always stood out to me was her homemade horchata. Horchata is a well-known and loved drink in Mexican culture, but every family has their own version, traditions, and special ingredients that make it unique. Through this project, I want to share not only my mom’s recipe, but also the warmth and connection that comes from cooking together. “La Receta de Mi Mamá,” which translates to “My Mom’s Recipe,” reflects the authenticity of our culture and the importance of passing traditions down through generations. What makes this website special is that it is for everyone. Kids can enjoy helping make the recipe, college students can reconnect with homemade comfort, and families can create memories together in the kitchen. As someone who grew up balancing both American life and my parents’ Mexican culture, I know many fellow Latinos hold their parents’ recipes close to their hearts. These recipes are more than food — they are memories, identity, and love. This website offers a small glimpse into our mama’s cocina — our mom’s kitchen — and invites others to experience a tradition that means so much to us. Whether someone is trying horchata for the first time or recreating flavors from their childhood, I hope this project reminds people that food is a love language meant to be shared with everyone.
