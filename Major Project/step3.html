Step 3: Sweetening & Flavoring | Tercer paso

Once the horchata base has been blended and strained, it is time to add the ingredients that create it's sweet and creamy flavor.

Instructions:

  1. Add milk to the strained horchata mixture.
  2. Slowly add sugar and stir well.
  3. Taste the horchata and adjust the sugar if needed.
  4. Add vanilla extract for additional flavor.
  5. Mix everything together until smooth and fully combined.

Adjusting the Sweetness-

The amount of sugar used can be adjusted depending on personal preference. Some people prefer a sweeter horchata, while others enjoy a lighter flavor.

Why Vanilla Matters-

Vanilla helps enhance the cinnamon and creamy flavors in the drink. Even a small amount adds warmth and richness to the final horchata.
