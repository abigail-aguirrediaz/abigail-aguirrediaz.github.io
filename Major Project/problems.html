Common Mistakes & Solution | Errores comunes

Making homemade horchata can take practice. Below are some common mistakes people experience and simple ways to fix them.

Grainy Texture-

If the horchata feels grainy, the mixture may not have been strained enough. Try straining the drink again using a fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth for a smoother texture.

Too Sweet-

If the horchata tastes too sweet, too much sugar may have been added. To balance the flavor, add more water or milk to dilute the sweetness.

Weak Flavor-

If the cinnamon flavor is weak, the mixture may not have contained enough cinnamon sticks or may not have soaked long enough. Adding more cinnamon can help strengthen the flavor.

Too Thick-

If the horchata is too thick, slowly add more water or milk until the drink reaches your preferred consistency.
