Step 1: Soaking the Rice | Primer Paso

The first step in making authentic horchata is preparing and soaking the rice mixture. This step is important because it softens the rice and helps release flavor from the cinnamon sticks.

Instructions:

  1. Rinse the rice thoroughly with water.
  2. Place the rice into a large bowl.
  3. Add cinnamon sticks and water to the bowl.
  4. Cover the bowl and allow the mixture to soak.

How Long Should It Soak?

For the best flavor and texture, soak the rice mixture for at least 4 hours. Overnight soaking is recommended because it softens the rice even more and creates a smoother horchata.

Why Soaking Matters-

Soaking allows the rice and cinnamon to absorb water, making them easier to blend later in the recipe. This step also helps create the creamy texture and rich cinnamon flavor that traditional horchata is known for.
