Pacific Rhododendron

Washington’s State Flower: The Pacific Rhododendron is also commonly known as California Rhododendron, or California Rosebay.According to the article "Native Plants PNW", the Pacific Rhododendron can be found west of the Cascades from southern British Columbia to Northern California. These plants are very tall reaching up to 24 ft (8m). The article "Coastal Interpretive Center", mentioned that the ideal climate for these plants would be in well-draining acidic soil with a low nitrogen content. They tend to grow in forests and even roasides. Those in higher locatins tend to bloom first. These beautiful flowers can be seen bloom between April and July.
